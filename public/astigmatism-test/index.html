FAQ: Online Astigmatism Test & Eye Care

What is astigmatism?

Astigmatism is a common, treatable imperfection in the curvature of your eye's cornea or lens. Instead of having a perfectly round shape (like a basketball), the eye curves more like a football, causing light to scatter and produce blurry or distorted vision at all distances.

How to test astigmatism at home?

Testing at home is simple with an online astigmatism test. By using a radiating line chart (such as our Siemens Star fan chart) and covering one eye, you can check if any lines appear darker or thicker. If they do, it may suggest the presence of uncorrected astigmatism.

Can astigmatism be cured?

Astigmatism cannot be cured naturally through eye exercises or diet. However, it can be corrected easily using prescription astigmatism glasses, toric contact lenses, or permanently fixed through surgical options like LASIK eye surgery.

Is online astigmatism test accurate?

An astigmatism test online serves as a preliminary screening and educational check astigmatism online tool. While highly helpful for highlighting visual errors, it does not replace a comprehensive, professional eye check with an eye care specialist.
